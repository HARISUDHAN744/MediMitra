# MediMitra External Data Sources and Licensing Registry

This document catalogues all official downloads, public datasets, and external APIs integrated into the MediMitra clinical platform. In compliance with fair-use and healthcare safety requirements, **no commercial pharmacy websites are scraped**.

| Provider / Dataset | Primary Use in MediMitra | Licensing & Terms | Attribution & Safety Notice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NPPA (DPCO 2013)** | Indian medicine ceiling price caps, NLEM list | Open Government Data (OGD) India / Gazette Notification | "As of Gazette Order Date; NPPA DPCO" |
| **PMBJP Jan Aushadhi** | Generic medicine catalog, PMBJP rates, savings calculation | Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Govt of India (Public Access) | "PMBJP Jan Aushadhi Rate Card" |
| **openFDA** | Structured Product Labels (indications, contraindications, interactions) | Open Government Public License / FDA terms | Quoted verbatim; marked "US label, may differ for India" |
| **NLM RxNav / RxNorm** | Active ingredient concept normalisation (RxCUI) | US National Library of Medicine (Public Domain) | "NLM RxNorm" |
| **DailyMed** | Secondary SPL drug label fallback | NLM / FDA (Public Domain) | "Attributed to DailyMed / FDA SPL" |
| **MedlinePlus** | Plain-language consumer health monographs | NLM / NIH (Public Domain) | "MedlinePlus Consumer Health Information" |
| **PubChem** | IUPAC names, molecular formulas, chemical structures | NLM / NIH (CC0 Public Domain) | "PubChem, NCBI" |
| **ChEMBL** | Mechanism of action, ATC classification, bioactivity data | EMBL-EBI (CC BY-SA 3.0) | "ChEMBL database, EMBL-EBI" |
| **OpenStreetMap / Overpass** | Nearby hospitals, clinics, 24/7 pharmacies, Jan Aushadhi Kendras | Open Database License (ODbL) | "© OpenStreetMap contributors" |
| **Nominatim** | Reverse and forward geocoding with 1 req/sec rate limit | Open Database License (ODbL) | "Geocoding © OpenStreetMap contributors" |
| **OSRM** | Real-world driving routes, distances, and ETAs | BSD 2-Clause License | "Routing provided via Project OSRM" |

## Ethical Scraping Policy
MediMitra explicitly refrains from scraping commercial e-pharmacy websites. All price, brand, and ingredient data originates from official public records, statutory ceiling notifications, and open scientific repositories.
