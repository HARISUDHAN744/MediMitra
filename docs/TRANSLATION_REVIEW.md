## Review Protocol
To mark a language as **Verified**:
1. A licensed medical doctor or clinical linguist fluent in the target language must review all 57 clinical UI strings.
2. Verify that drug frequency terms (e.g. *twice daily*, *before food*) and safety notices cannot be misunderstood.
3. Record the reviewer's full name, credentials, date, and commit SHA in this document.
